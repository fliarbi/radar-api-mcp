# Keyword research for the use-case pages

[All use cases](../use-cases/README.md) · [Raw data](keyword-research-2026-10-16.json)

Research date **2026-10-16**. Average monthly searches (third-party keyword-tool estimates; the tool's monthly series lags about 12 months). Zero means below the tool's threshold or not found.

Each candidate topic was expanded into two search phrases; demand score = summed India + US monthly searches across its phrases. Pages are built in score order; a topic is kept only when its captured API responses are populated.

Status: **page** = built; **thin** = captured but below the populated threshold; **excluded** = overlap or ambiguous query; **not reached** = the segment already had its pages.
